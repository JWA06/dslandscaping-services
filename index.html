What we do

Everything your garden needs

Whether it needs regular care or a fresh new look, we’ll take care of the hard work for you.

Garden Maintenance

Regular or one-off visits to weed, prune, tidy borders and keep everything looking its best all year round.

Lawn Care & Turfing

Mowing, edging, feeding and scarifying, plus fresh new turf laid properly for a lush, healthy lawn.

Hedge & Tree Trimming

Neat, shapely hedges and well-kept shrubs and small trees, with all the cuttings cleared away.

Patios & Paving

New patios, paths and edging that give you a smart, practical space to relax and entertain.

Fencing & Decking

Sturdy fences, gates and decking, fitted to last and finished to complement your garden.

Planting & Garden Design

Fresh borders, beds and planting schemes designed around your space, your style and your budget.
