Our services

What we can do for your garden

Everything from regular upkeep to bigger projects. We also take on garden clearances and smaller odd jobs, so if it’s not listed, just ask.

01

Garden Maintenance

Regular or one-off visits: weeding, pruning, borders and general upkeep to keep everything looking its best.

02

Lawn Care & Turfing

Mowing, edging, feeding and scarifying, or a brand-new lawn laid on properly prepared ground.

03

Hedge Cutting & Shaping

Hedges and shrubs cut back, shaped and kept in check, with every clipping cleared away.

04

Patios & Paving

Patios, paths and edging laid on a solid base, in natural stone or porcelain.

05

Fencing & Gates

New fence panels, posts and gates fitted to last, plus repairs to storm-damaged fencing.

06

Planting & Borders

New beds and borders planned and planted, or tired ones lifted and given a fresh start.

Every garden is different. We’ll come and see yours, talk it through and give you a clear, honest quote.
