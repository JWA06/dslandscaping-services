FAQs

Questions, answered

Can’t see what you’re looking for? Give us a call on 07700 900123 and we’ll be happy to help.

Do you charge for quotes?

No. We’ll come and look at the garden, talk through what you’d like and give you a clear quote. There’s no obligation to go ahead.

Do you take the garden waste away?

Yes. Grass cuttings, hedge clippings and other green waste are cleared up and taken away, so you’re left with a tidy garden.

Can you help with a one-off job?

Of course. As well as regular visits, we take on one-off jobs like hedge cutting, garden clearances and tidy-ups before a sale or a special occasion.

How often should my lawn be cut?

During the main growing season, roughly April to October, most lawns look their best cut every week or two. We can set up regular visits so you never have to think about it.

When is the best time to cut a hedge?

Most garden hedges are best trimmed in late summer or early autumn. Birds nest in hedges between March and August, so we always check carefully before cutting during those months.

Which areas do you cover?

We’re based in Your Town and cover the surrounding area. If you’re not sure whether you’re in range, just ask.
